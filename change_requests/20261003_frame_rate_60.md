# Frame rate 24 to 60

## Why
- Amp-driven motion looked jumpy. The param update rate was not the cause:
  `APP_PARAM_UPDATE_RATE` is already `1`, so `shouldUpdateParams()` returns true every frame.
- The ceiling was `APP_FRAME_RATE = 24`. `rms.analyze()` was being sampled 24 times a second, so
  any motion driven by it stepped regardless of smoothing.
- `frameRate()` in Processing is a target, not a lock. If the machine cannot hold 60, it runs below
  60 rather than failing.

## What / How
- `Util.pde` — `APP_FRAME_RATE` `24` → `60`.

## Does this change what appears on screen?
- [x] Yes — a frame rate.

## Dependencies
- [x] No new library, no new asset in `data/`

## Out of Scope
- `APP_PARAM_UPDATE_RATE`, already 1. Untouched.
- `smoothingFactorAmp` (`AudioInput.pde:8`, currently `0.70`) and `smoothingFactorFFT`
  (`AudioInput.pde:9`, currently `0.90`). Both are per-frame 1-pole constants tuned by eye at 24fps,
  so at 60fps they smooth roughly 2.5x less in wall-clock time and will read tighter and less laggy.
  Retuning them is a separate judgement after seeing this run.
- `ParamSmoothener`, which derives its coefficient from `frameRate()` and so also shifts with this
  change. Same retuning caveat.
- The bloom implementation.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Sketch opens in the Processing IDE and runs.
- [ ] Amp-driven motion reads smooth rather than stepped.
- [ ] If it now reads jittery rather than laggy, `smoothingFactorAmp` is the knob, not the frame rate.

## Note
- 60 is the standard display rate and was chosen as the default. `APP_FRAME_RATE` is the only thing
  that needs changing to try 30, 50, or 120.
